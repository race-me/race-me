The seat-time program

Wrench time earns seat time

Every hour you put into a club car earns credit toward driving it. Seats at each event go to the members who built the car, and drivers split the cost of tires, fuel, and the entry fee. Credit is good for 12 months.

Why we're a nonprofit

Nobody gets rich here, and that's the point

Race Me is a 501(c)(7) social club, owned and run by its members. There's no owner taking a cut, so dues go straight back into what we do together.

The annual run

One big road trip a year

In 2025 we drove about 2,000 miles to the Pacific Coast Highway and back. In 2026 we went bigger: 3,577 miles, 8 days, and 15 states to drive the Tail of the Dragon, 318 curves in 11 miles. Every member drives, and every car makes it home. Mostly.

Our affiliate team

Race Me members also race with Dadbod Carmod LLC, our affiliate team out of Denver. In 2026 the crew took back-to-back class wins at 24 Hours of Lemons at High Plains Raceway: the MR2 in Class B at the B.F.E. GP in June, then the Yaris in Class C at the High Plains Drifter, a full 24-hour race, on September 26.
