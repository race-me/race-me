Affiliate team · Denver Back-to-back class wins · 2026

Dadbod Carmod MR2

Race Me members also race with Dadbod Carmod LLC, our affiliate team out of Denver, and the crew took back-to-back class wins at 24 Hours of Lemons in 2026. In June at the B.F.E. GP at High Plains Raceway, the #177 MR2 ran 336 laps in 14.5 hours and took the Class B win by two clean laps. Its stablemate, the green #157 Yaris, led Class C until a brake failure cost a three-hour repair. Both cars came back for the High Plains Drifter, a full 24-hour race, on September 26: the MR2 as #75 and the Yaris as #57. This time the Yaris took the Class C win by 49 laps and finished 5th overall, with 522 laps in 24 hours and a best lap of 2:14.337 (68.3 mph).
