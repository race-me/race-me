One Lap of America

Tire Rack One Lap of America is the week-long event Brock Yates started: one car, driven on the street from track to track across the country and timed at every stop. It starts at Tire Rack in South Bend, Indiana, and the car you drive there is the car you race, so it has to survive the highway as well as the track.

Race Me members run it in Dadbod Carmod #3, an orange 2006 Lotus Elise wearing #64, with E. Pilarczyk and G. Young on the doors.
